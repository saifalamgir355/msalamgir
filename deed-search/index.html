Lost the deed? The registry still has it.

দলিল তল্লাশি ও সার্টিফাইড নকল

Every registered deed is copied into the Sub-Registry volumes and stays there. If your original is lost, damaged or held by someone else, a certified copy carries the same information — and a search can uncover the chain deeds you never had.

When people need a deed search

  • The original deed is lost, burnt or water-damaged.
  • A relative holds the original and will not hand it over.
  • You are buying and need the seller's chain (bia) deeds, which he no longer has.
  • A bank, court or land office has asked for a certified copy.
  • You suspect a deed shown to you is forged and want the registry's own version.

What we need from you

  • Mouza name and dag number, and the khatian if you have it.
  • Approximate year of registration, and the deed number if known.
  • Names of the buyer and seller as they would appear on the deed.
  • Which Sub-Registry office the land falls under.

The less you know, the longer the search takes — old volumes are indexed by name and year, so a rough date range helps a great deal.

How it works

  1. Index searchWe search the Sub-Registry indexes by name, year and mouza to locate the volume and page.
  2. Application for the copyA formal application with the prescribed fee for a certified copy (নকল).
  3. Collection and checkWe collect the copy and read it against your khatian so you know it matches the land you think it covers.

What a certified copy can and cannot do

A certified copy proves what was registered and is accepted by banks, land offices and courts. It does not by itself prove current ownership — for that you still need the chain of deeds and the khatian. If you are verifying a purchase, pair the search with khatian and dag verification.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a copy of a deed if I lost the original?

Yes. Registered deeds are copied into the Sub-Registry volumes permanently. With the mouza, dag and an approximate year we can search the index and apply for a certified copy.
মূল দলিল হারিয়ে গেলে কি নকল পাওয়া যায়? হ্যাঁ। রেজিস্ট্রিকৃত দলিল সাব-রেজিস্ট্রি অফিসের ভলিউমে স্থায়ীভাবে নকল থাকে। মৌজা, দাগ ও আনুমানিক সন জানা থাকলে সূচি তল্লাশি করে সার্টিফাইড নকলের আবেদন করা যায়।

How long does a deed search take?

It depends on how much you know and how old the deed is. A recent deed with a known number can be quick; an old deed with only a name and a decade takes longer.
দলিল তল্লাশিতে কত সময় লাগে? আপনি কতটুকু তথ্য দিতে পারছেন এবং দলিল কত পুরনো তার উপর নির্ভর করে। নম্বর জানা থাকলে দ্রুত হয়; শুধু নাম ও আনুমানিক দশক জানা থাকলে সময় বেশি লাগে।

Is a certified copy as good as the original?

For proving what was registered, yes — banks, land offices and courts accept it. It does not replace the chain of deeds and khatian needed to prove present ownership.
সার্টিফাইড নকল কি মূল দলিলের সমান? কী রেজিস্ট্রি হয়েছিল তা প্রমাণের জন্য হ্যাঁ — ব্যাংক, ভূমি অফিস ও আদালত এটি গ্রহণ করে। তবে বর্তমান মালিকানা প্রমাণে বায়া দলিল ও খতিয়ানও প্রয়োজন।
