Check a khatian and dag before you trust a deed.

খতিয়ান ও দাগ যাচাইয়ের নিয়ম

Every plot in Bangladesh has a record history — CS, SA, RS and BS khatian. Reading them in order is how you find out whether the person selling the land really owns it.

The four khatian surveys, in order

  • CS (Cadastral Survey) — the oldest record, from British times. The starting point of the chain.
  • SA (State Acquisition) — prepared after zamindari abolition, usually without fresh field survey.
  • RS (Revisional Survey) — a fresh field survey; generally the most reliable older record.
  • BS / City Survey — the latest survey, used in Chattogram city areas.

A clean title shows the same ownership line running through all four, matching the deeds. A break anywhere in that chain is where disputes start.

What to check, step by step

  1. Collect the recordsGet the khatian copies and the mouza map sheet for the dag number.
  2. Match names and sharesThe seller's name and share must appear in the latest khatian, not only in an old one.
  3. Follow the chain deedsEach transfer from the recorded owner down to the seller must exist and be registered.
  4. Check the mutationThe mutation khatian and DCR should already be in the seller's name.
  5. Measure on the groundThe dag's area on the map must match what is being sold and what is physically there.

Online records, and their limits

The Ministry of Land publishes khatian and mouza map services on land.gov.bd and its e-porcha service. They are useful for a first look, but the online copy is not always complete or current, and it does not tell you whether the land on the ground matches the record. For a purchase, verify at the land office and measure the plot. We do both — see land & GPS survey and property legal help.

Signs of a forged or risky record

  • Name, date or dag number on the deed does not match the Sub-Registry volume.
  • Khatian still in a dead owner's name with no warish certificate.
  • Area in the deed larger than the dag can hold.
  • Seller offers only photocopies and avoids the land office.

Frequently asked questions

How can I check a khatian online?

The Ministry of Land publishes khatian and mouza map services on land.gov.bd and e-porcha. Search by division, district, upazila, mouza and khatian or dag number. Treat the result as a first check, not proof of ownership.
অনলাইনে খতিয়ান কীভাবে যাচাই করব? ভূমি মন্ত্রণালয়ের land.gov.bd ও ই-পর্চা সেবায় বিভাগ, জেলা, উপজেলা, মৌজা এবং খতিয়ান বা দাগ নম্বর দিয়ে খোঁজা যায়। তবে এটি প্রাথমিক যাচাই — মালিকানার চূড়ান্ত প্রমাণ নয়।

What is the difference between CS, SA, RS and BS khatian?

They are four successive land surveys. CS is the oldest, SA followed zamindari abolition, RS was a fresh field survey, and BS or city survey is the latest. A good title runs unbroken through all of them.
সি.এস, এস.এ, আর.এস ও বি.এস খতিয়ানের পার্থক্য কী? এগুলো পর্যায়ক্রমে চারটি জরিপ। সি.এস সবচেয়ে পুরনো, এস.এ জমিদারি প্রথা বিলোপের পর, আর.এস নতুন সরেজমিন জরিপ এবং বি.এস বা সিটি জরিপ সর্বশেষ। ভালো মালিকানায় চারটিতেই ধারাবাহিকতা থাকে।

Can you verify records for land outside Chattogram?

We work across Chattogram district. For land elsewhere we can review the papers you send and tell you what to check locally.
চট্টগ্রামের বাইরের জমির রেকর্ড যাচাই করেন? আমরা চট্টগ্রাম জেলা জুড়ে কাজ করি। অন্য জেলার জমির ক্ষেত্রে আপনার পাঠানো কাগজপত্র দেখে কী যাচাই করতে হবে তা জানিয়ে দিতে পারি।
