What is namjari?

Namjari (also called mutation or kharij) updates the government land record — the khatian — to show the new owner after a sale, gift, inheritance or partition. Without it you cannot pay land tax in your name, and a later sale or bank loan becomes difficult.

Documents usually needed

  • Certified copy of the registered deed (or warish certificate for inherited land).
  • Latest khatian copy and the chain of previous deeds.
  • Up-to-date land tax (khajna) receipt.
  • Applicant’s NID and photograph.
  • An active mobile number for e-namjari notifications.

The e-namjari process

  1. Online applicationThe application is submitted through the government e-namjari system with scanned documents.
  2. VerificationThe union or municipal land office checks the records and reports to the AC (Land).
  3. HearingThe applicant attends a hearing at the AC (Land) office with original documents.
  4. Approval & DCRAfter approval the fee is paid online and the DCR and new khatian are issued.

The government fee and service timeline are set by the Ministry of Land and published on land.gov.bd. We confirm the current figures when we take your case.

Record correction

If the existing khatian has a wrong name, area or dag number, it may need correction first. We identify this during document review and file the correct application.

Frequently asked questions

How long does namjari take?

It depends on the land office and on whether the papers are complete. The Ministry of Land sets a service timeline; we give you a realistic estimate after checking your documents.
নামজারিতে কত দিন লাগে? ভূমি অফিস ও কাগজপত্র সম্পূর্ণ কিনা তার উপর নির্ভর করে। ভূমি মন্ত্রণালয় সময়সীমা নির্ধারণ করে; কাগজ দেখে আমরা বাস্তবসম্মত সময় জানাই।

Can namjari be done online?

Yes. Applications are made through the government e-namjari system, but a hearing at the AC (Land) office with original papers is still required.
নামজারি কি অনলাইনে করা যায়? হ্যাঁ। ই-নামজারি পদ্ধতিতে অনলাইনে আবেদন হয়, তবে মূল কাগজসহ এসি (ল্যান্ড) অফিসে শুনানিতে উপস্থিত থাকতে হয়।

What is a DCR?

The DCR (Duplicate Carbon Receipt) is the receipt issued after the mutation fee is paid. Together with the new khatian, it proves the mutation is complete.
ডিসিআর কী? নামজারি ফি পরিশোধের পর যে রসিদ দেওয়া হয় তাকে ডিসিআর বলে। নতুন খতিয়ানের সাথে এটি নামজারি সম্পন্ন হওয়ার প্রমাণ।
