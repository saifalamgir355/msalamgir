Ten things to check before you pay for land.

জমি কেনার আগে ১০টি করণীয়

Almost every land dispute we see could have been avoided for a fraction of its cost — by checking the papers before the advance was paid. Work through this list before you commit to anything.

The checklist

  1. See the original deed, not a photocopyAsk for the seller's original title deed and compare it against the Sub-Registry volume.
  2. Follow the chain backEvery transfer from the recorded owner down to the seller must exist and be registered. A missing link is a dispute waiting to happen.
  3. Read all four khatianCS, SA, RS and BS should show the same ownership line. See how to check khatian and dag.
  4. Confirm the mutationThe mutation khatian and DCR should already be in the seller's name — not his father's, not a company's.
  5. Check the khajna receiptLand tax paid up to the current year, in the seller's name.
  6. Measure the landThe area on the ground must match the deed and the mouza map. Encroachment and shortfall are common — see land and GPS survey.
  7. Identify every heirIf the land was inherited, all heirs must sell or consent. A sale by some heirs can be challenged for years.
  8. Ask about cases, mortgages and acquisitionCheck for pending suits, bank mortgage, and whether any government acquisition notice touches the plot.
  9. Put the advance in writingA registered baina (sale agreement) with a clear deadline protects you far better than a cash receipt.
  10. Budget the full costStamp duty, registration fee, local tax, source tax and mutation all come on top of the land price.

Warning signs to walk away from

  • The seller will only show photocopies and avoids the land office.
  • The price is well below the area rate and there is pressure to pay quickly.
  • Names, dates or dag numbers differ between the deed and the khatian.
  • The person selling holds a power of attorney but will not show the registered original.
  • Neighbours describe the boundary differently from the seller.

Frequently asked questions

What should I check before paying the baina?

The seller’s original deed, the chain deeds, all four khatian, the mutation and DCR, an up-to-date khajna receipt, and a physical measurement of the land. Check all of it before any money changes hands.
বায়না দেওয়ার আগে কী কী যাচাই করব? বিক্রেতার মূল দলিল, বায়া দলিল, চারটি খতিয়ান, নামজারি ও ডিসিআর, হালনাগাদ খাজনার রসিদ এবং সরেজমিন পরিমাপ। টাকা দেওয়ার আগেই সব যাচাই করুন।

Is a baina agreement necessary?

Strongly recommended. A registered sale agreement records the price, the deadline and what happens if either side backs out. A plain cash receipt gives you very little.
বায়নানামা কি জরুরি? অবশ্যই করা উচিত। রেজিস্ট্রিকৃত বায়নানামায় মূল্য, সময়সীমা এবং কোনো পক্ষ সরে গেলে কী হবে তা লেখা থাকে। শুধু নগদ রসিদে সুরক্ষা খুব কম।

How long does a pre-purchase check take?

Usually a few days, depending on how quickly the land office and Sub-Registry produce records. We tell you the timeline after seeing your papers.
কেনার আগে যাচাইয়ে কত সময় লাগে? সাধারণত কয়েক দিন — ভূমি অফিস ও সাব-রেজিস্ট্রি থেকে রেকর্ড পেতে কত সময় লাগে তার উপর নির্ভর করে। কাগজ দেখে আমরা সময় জানিয়ে দিই।
