What our legal help covers

  • Title verification before buying — seller’s deed, chain (bia) deeds back to the original record, CS/SA/RS/BS and city-survey khatian, mutation khatian and DCR.
  • Flat purchase review — landowner–developer agreement, registered power of attorney, approved CDA plan and the allotment terms.
  • Inheritance & partition — warish certificate, share calculation among heirs and a registered bontonnama (partition deed).
  • Record & mutation correction — wrong names, areas or dag numbers in the khatian, corrected through the AC (Land) office.
  • Agreements & power of attorney — baina (sale agreement), power of attorney, heba (gift) and exchange deeds in proper legal form.
  • Dispute documentation — boundary and ownership disputes backed by survey maps and certified records.

Red flags we check for

Before you pay an advance (baina), we look for the problems that most often cost buyers their money:

  • The same land already sold to someone else, or mortgaged to a bank.
  • Forged deeds, or names, dates and dag numbers that do not match the volume at the Sub-Registry.
  • Khatian or mutation not in the seller’s name.
  • Heirs who have not signed — a sale by only some heirs can be challenged later.
  • Less land on the ground than the deed says — confirmed by measurement.
  • Unpaid land tax (khajna), acquisition notices or pending cases on the plot.

When you need an advocate

Our legal help is given as a government-licensed deed writer and surveyor. If a matter has to go to court — a title suit, partition suit or injunction — we refer you to a trusted enrolled advocate and hand over the full paperwork and survey evidence, so the case starts on solid ground.

Frequently asked questions

Can you check land papers before I pay the baina?

Yes — that is the best time. Send copies of the seller’s deed and khatian on WhatsApp and we will tell you what needs checking before any money changes hands.
বায়না দেওয়ার আগে কি কাগজ যাচাই করে দেন? হ্যাঁ — সেটাই সবচেয়ে উপযুক্ত সময়। বিক্রেতার দলিল ও খতিয়ানের কপি হোয়াটসঅ্যাপে পাঠান, টাকা দেওয়ার আগে কী যাচাই করতে হবে জানিয়ে দেব।

Can you tell if a deed is fake?

We compare the deed with the Sub-Registry volume, chain deeds, khatian and mutation records. Mismatches in names, dates, dag numbers or area are the usual warning signs.
দলিল জাল কিনা বুঝতে পারেন? সাব-রেজিস্ট্রি অফিসের ভলিউম, বায়া দলিল, খতিয়ান ও নামজারি রেকর্ডের সাথে দলিল মিলিয়ে দেখি। নাম, তারিখ, দাগ নম্বর বা পরিমাণে অমিলই সাধারণ সতর্ক সংকেত।

Do you represent clients in court?

No. Court representation is done by enrolled advocates. We prepare the documents and survey evidence and refer you to a trusted advocate when a case is needed.
আপনারা কি আদালতে মামলা পরিচালনা করেন? না। আদালতে প্রতিনিধিত্ব করেন নিবন্ধিত আইনজীবী। আমরা কাগজপত্র ও জরিপ প্রমাণ প্রস্তুত করি এবং প্রয়োজনে বিশ্বস্ত আইনজীবীর কাছে পাঠাই।
