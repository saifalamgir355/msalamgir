New · Property Legal Help

Buy, sell or inherit —
know you’re legally safe.

Most land problems start with a paper nobody checked. Before you pay, sign or divide a property, we verify every record and tell you plainly where the risks are — then prepare the documents to fix them.

Red flags we check for

  • Same land sold to two buyers
  • Forged or mismatched deeds
  • Khatian not in the seller’s name
  • Heirs left out of a sale
  • Land area less than the deed says
  • Unpaid khajna or pending cases

Title & khatian verification

Seller’s deed, chain (bia) deeds, CS/SA/RS/BS khatian, mutation and DCR checked against land-office records.

Flat purchase review

Landowner–developer agreement, power of attorney and CDA-approved plan reviewed before you book or register a flat.

Inheritance & partition

Warish shares worked out, family agreement documented and a registered bontonnama so every heir’s share is secure.

Record & mutation correction

Wrong names, areas or plot numbers in the khatian corrected through the proper AC (Land) process.

Agreements & power of attorney

Baina (sale agreement), power of attorney, heba and exchange deeds drafted in correct legal form.

Dispute documentation

Boundary and ownership disputes backed by survey maps and records. Where a court case is needed, we refer you to a trusted advocate.

Legal help is provided as a licensed deed writer and surveyor. Court representation is handled by enrolled advocates we refer you to.

      Ask a question
      Do you survey hilly land?

      Yes. Land and hill measurement is one of our core services, and GPS survey makes large and hilly plots faster and more accurate to map.

      What is the difference between a normal survey and a GPS survey?

      A tape or chain survey measures lengths and area. A GPS survey also records the exact coordinates of each boundary pillar, so the boundary can be found again precisely in future.

      How long do registration and mutation take?

      It depends on your papers and on the government office’s schedule. After reviewing your documents we give you a realistic timeline and keep you updated at every step.

      Can you check a property before I buy it?

      Yes. We verify the seller’s deed, chain deeds, khatian and mutation records, and measure the land if needed — so you know exactly what you are buying.

      Do you give legal help on land and property matters?

      Yes. We verify deeds and khatian, prepare agreements, power of attorney and partition deeds, correct records and document disputes. If a matter has to go to court, we refer you to a trusted enrolled advocate and give them the full paperwork.

      Can you tell if a deed is fake or the land was sold twice?

      We cross-check the deed with Sub-Registry volumes, the chain deeds, khatian and mutation records. Mismatches in names, dates, dag numbers or area are the usual signs — we point them out before you pay anything. See how khatian and dag verification works.

      Do you handle flat registration for buyers?

      Yes. We review the developer agreement, power of attorney and approved plan, then draft and register the flat deed and complete the mutation in your name.

      Do you work with companies as well as families?

      Yes. Our clients include a real estate developer, a ship recycling yard, a depot and logistics company, an agribusiness group and a food manufacturer, alongside hundreds of individual landowners.
