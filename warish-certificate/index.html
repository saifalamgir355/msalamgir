Inherited land: the warish certificate comes first.

ওয়ারিশ সনদ ও বণ্টননামা

When an owner dies, the land does not automatically become any one heir’s. Until the heirs are recorded and the shares divided, nobody can sell, mutate or mortgage cleanly. The warish certificate is where that starts.

What a warish certificate is

A warish (succession) certificate is the official list of a deceased person's legal heirs, issued locally — by the union parishad chairman in rural areas, or by the ward councillor in a city corporation area such as Chattogram. It names every heir and their relationship to the deceased. Every later step depends on it being complete and correct.

Documents usually required

  • Death certificate of the owner.
  • NID of the applicant and of each heir.
  • Birth certificates where a relationship needs proof.
  • The deceased's land documents — deed, khatian, khajna receipt.
  • Application in the prescribed form to the chairman or councillor.

The steps after the certificate

  1. Work out the sharesShares follow the personal law that applies to the family. Getting this wrong is the single most common cause of family land disputes.
  2. Agree the divisionWho takes which portion, and whether anyone is compensated in cash for a smaller share.
  3. Register a bontonnamaA registered partition deed makes the agreed division legally binding. A verbal family arrangement does not survive a disagreement.
  4. Mutate each shareEach heir files namjari for their own portion so the khatian and khajna follow the new owners.

Why a registered bontonnama matters

Families often divide land informally and live that way for decades. It holds until someone wants to sell, mortgage or build — then the buyer's lawyer asks for the partition deed and there isn't one. Registering it while everyone is on good terms costs a fraction of what a partition suit costs later.

We prepare warish applications, calculate shares, draft and register the bontonnama, and complete the mutation for each heir. If a dispute has already begun, see property legal help.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get a warish certificate in Chattogram?

In a city corporation area, from your ward councillor; in rural areas, from the union parishad chairman. You apply with the death certificate, NIDs of the heirs and the deceased’s land papers.
চট্টগ্রামে ওয়ারিশ সনদ কোথায় পাওয়া যায়? সিটি কর্পোরেশন এলাকায় ওয়ার্ড কাউন্সিলরের কাছ থেকে, গ্রামে ইউনিয়ন পরিষদ চেয়ারম্যানের কাছ থেকে। মৃত্যু সনদ, ওয়ারিশদের জাতীয় পরিচয়পত্র ও জমির কাগজসহ আবেদন করতে হয়।

Can one heir sell inherited land alone?

An heir can only transfer his or her own share. Selling the whole property without the other heirs’ consent can be challenged, and most careful buyers will refuse.
একজন ওয়ারিশ কি একাই জমি বিক্রি করতে পারেন? একজন ওয়ারিশ কেবল নিজের অংশ হস্তান্তর করতে পারেন। অন্য ওয়ারিশদের সম্মতি ছাড়া পুরো সম্পত্তি বিক্রি করলে তা চ্যালেঞ্জ হতে পারে এবং সতর্ক ক্রেতা সাধারণত রাজি হন না।

Is a bontonnama compulsory?

Not legally compulsory, but without a registered partition deed each heir’s share is not recorded separately — which blocks mutation, sale and bank loans later.
বণ্টননামা কি বাধ্যতামূলক? আইনত বাধ্যতামূলক নয়, তবে রেজিস্ট্রিকৃত বণ্টননামা ছাড়া প্রত্যেক ওয়ারিশের অংশ আলাদাভাবে লিপিবদ্ধ হয় না — ফলে পরে নামজারি, বিক্রি ও ব্যাংক ঋণে সমস্যা হয়।
